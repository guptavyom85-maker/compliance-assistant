# Demonstration walkthrough

Approximate total runtime: 1 minute 35 seconds.

## 1. Ask a question - 30 seconds

Demonstration question:

> What proportion of individual traders in the equity F&O segment incurred losses according to the SEBI study of January 2023?

| Time | On-screen action | Voice-over |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-4 sec | Open **Ask Question**. | "This is the Ask Question workspace, where users obtain answers grounded in uploaded compliance documents." |
| 4-9 sec | Enter the demonstration question. | "I begin by entering a specific regulatory or compliance-related question." |
| 9-14 sec | Select **SEBI derivatives**. | "Next, I select the exact document the assistant may reference, preventing unrelated sources from influencing the answer." |
| 14-18 sec | Show **Semantic search** and **Automatic**. | "Semantic search finds passages by meaning, while the automatic strategy handles retrieval and answer generation." |
| 18-20 sec | Select **Get Answer**, then cut to a completed result. | "I then select Get Answer." |
| 20-30 sec | Show the answer, confidence, warning, and citations. | "The system retrieves relevant passages, generates a grounded response, verifies its claims, and displays citations, evidence confidence, and any human-review warning. The interaction is also saved in the Query Log." |

## Recording notes

- Generate the demonstration answer before recording and cut from **Get Answer** to the completed result.
- Do not wait for indexing during the video; explain the operation and show an already indexed document.
- Keep API keys, local file paths, browser bookmarks, and personal notifications off screen.
- If the external model fails, do not present the error screen as the intended answer flow. Re-record after a successful response.
